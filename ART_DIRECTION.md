# Cloud Junction visual contract

The seven reference images committed on 20 September 2026 are the visual source of truth for future world-building. They are references for mood, shape language, palette, density, and composition—not assets to reproduce literally.

## Non-negotiable silhouette language

- Trains are long, rounded, handcrafted machines: capsule-ended carriages, layered boilers, visible roof ribs, warm windows, dark underframes, brass/copper trim, and painted orange, oxblood, teal, cream, or deep blue metal.
- Major locomotives should feel assembled from overlapping tanks, domes, smokestacks, bands, rivets, cabins, and wheel housings. Avoid a single box with wheels.
- Passenger stock should read as a continuous elegant ribbon when seen in profile, like the orange railcar reference, while every carriage continues to follow the curve independently.
- Floating islands have broad living crowns, irregular natural edges, tapered rock bodies, secondary stone spires, trees, and architecture. Never ship a bare cylinder or perfect disc as a finished island.
- Grand Cloud Junction is a dense vertical sky settlement: clustered cream towers, copper/teal roofs, aerial bridges, hanging transit, airships, rail layers, gardens, and luminous windows.
- Clouds form large sculpted banks and a continuous sea. Small isolated white balls are supporting detail only.

## Composition

The normal chase view must retain a three-quarter view of the selected train and show its window band. It should also reveal curved track, cloud depth, and at least one reachable landmark. A city or island can frame the foreground, but it must not permanently hide the train or railway.

## Guardrails

- Keep the palette warm and painterly; reserve pure black for underframes and deep mechanical recesses.
- Prefer rounded, tapered, layered, and asymmetrical forms over repeated cubes.
- Add density at landmarks, not uniformly across the whole world.
- Preserve the existing mobile quality tiers, six-train simulation, independent car following, and graceful WebGL failure path when adding detail.
- Do not copy identifiable characters, exact vehicles, or exact buildings from any reference.
